These are the initial code files which to a point were unreleased and were being developed during the testing and design of a product
for Modbus gateway availability to monitor and control them by writing to the device registers with mapped commands over the internet.
for gateway purposes all the internet stream that are conventionally available were incoroporated (WiFi-LAN/ETH-GPRS). Hardware consist
an ESP32, MAC/ETH ICs, SIM800c Modem and other supporting circuits of RS232 & RS485 over UARTs, I2C, SPI and other I/O to poll and
and send. Along with that product was actually designed for solar plant control, as an alternative to Net-metering equipment. That
functionality enabled limiting the inverter out till the load generation only or exporting as per the desired number of available 
energy remaining.
